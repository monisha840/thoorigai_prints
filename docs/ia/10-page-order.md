# 10 · Recommended Page Order

Two different orderings matter, and they are not the same list.

1. **Build order** — the sequence pages are designed, written, and shipped in.
2. **Presentation order** — the sequence a visitor meets them in, on the site and in the navigation.

---

## Part 1 · Build order

The ordering principle is **conversion value per unit of blocked content**. Pages that convert and are not waiting on a photo shoot come first; pages waiting on content nobody has collected yet come last, however appealing they are to design.

### Wave 0 · Before any page is built

Not pages, but nothing should be designed until these clear. Two of them are live liabilities.

## Part 2 · Presentation order

### Navigation order

```
Printing   Packaging   Binding   Materials   Work   About
```

| Position | Item | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | The three pillars, in production sequence | Print → package → bind is how the business actually reads; it also puts the highest-volume pillar first |
| 4 | Materials | Sits after the pillars because it is a *how*, not a *what* |
| 5 | Work | Proof follows capability. Reserved even before it launches. |
| 6 | About | Last, because the founder story converts people who are already interested |

Contact is not in the nav; the persistent CTA, sticky bar and footer carry it. Process, FAQ and Journal live in the footer — they are arrived at with a reason, not browsed.

### Homepage section order

Narrative, not catalogue: **what → proof → what exactly → how → who → act.**

```
 1  Hero                    what we do, where we are
 2  Credibility strip       are they real
 3  Three pillars           do they do my thing
 4  Press sequence          can they actually make it        ← 3D signature
 5  Catalogue preview       what exactly can I order
 6  Materials teaser        how premium does this get        ← 3D
 7  Founder                 who am I dealing with
 8  Commitments             why them
 9  Work                    proof                            ← hidden until real
10  Client logos            proof                            ← hidden until real
11  FAQ                     last objections
12  Conversion block        act
```

The two hidden sections collapse cleanly: with both absent the page runs 1–8 then 11–12 and still reads as a complete argument. That is the test every conditional section must pass.

### Capability page section order

The buyer's questions, in the order they ask them:

```
Can you do it at all?   →  Key specs (four pinned facts)
What is it?             →  What it is
What will it look like? →  Interactive viewer
What are my options?    →  Choose your options
Exactly what specs?     →  Full specification
Is it right for me?     →  Best for
What about the other?   →  Compared to
Prove it                →  Products · Work
Anything else?          →  FAQ
Start                   →  Conversion block
```

Specs come before the 3D deliberately. A buyer whose quantity is outside the range should find that out in five seconds rather than after a WebGL scene loads.

## Part 3 · Dependency map

What blocks what, at a glance. The critical path runs through copy and the logo, not through 3D.

```
LOGO AS SVG ─────────────► header · footer · favicon · loader
                                    │
COPY (the long pole) ──────────────►│──► every page ships
   15 page bodies                   │
   43 product descriptions          │
   24 titles + descriptions         │
   168 alt attributes               │
                                    ▼
CANONICAL PHONE ────────► siteConfig ──► every CTA, schema, WhatsApp
BUSINESS HOURS ─────────►     │
GSTIN ──────────────────►     │
                              ▼
                       WAVE 1 · quote · contact · home · 404
                              │
                              ▼
                       WAVE 2 · three pillar hubs
                              │
                              ▼
                       WAVE 3 · 13 capability pages + 43 catalogue entries
                              │
FOUNDER PHOTO ──────────►     │
FACILITY PHOTO ─────────►     ▼
MACHINE LIST ───────────► WAVE 4 · about · process · faq · legal
                              │
PRODUCT RE-SHOOT ───────►     ▼
MATERIAL TEXTURES ──────► WAVE 5 · materials · configurator · sequences
                              │
CASE STUDIES ───────────►     ▼
REAL TESTIMONIALS ──────► WAVE 6 · work · trust sections · journal
CLIENT LOGOS ───────────►
```

**Three things gate more than anything else:** the SVG logo, the copy, and the product re-shoot. Nine in-use product photographs are under 700px wide and cannot survive a premium layout, a lightbox, or a 3D texture map. Commission the shoot in week 1; it is the input with the longest lead time and the widest blast radius.

## Part 4 · What each wave gives the client

Useful for staging the engagement, and for keeping the sequence honest when someone asks for the configurator first.

| After wave | The site can do this |
|---|---|
| **0** | Stops embarrassing itself: no fake testimonials, no Lorem Ipsum, no AI-course landing page, one working phone number |
| **1** | Actually captures leads. A real quote page, a contact page with a form, a homepage that says what the business does and where. |
| **2** | Routes buyers correctly. Three hubs that explain a decision instead of repeating a photo grid. |
| **3** | Answers the specification buyer. Specs, MOQs, turnarounds, comparisons — the content the whole category is missing. |
| **4** | Earns trust. Founder, facility, process, machines, FAQ, and a privacy policy that exists. |
| **5** | Becomes distinctive. The 3D nobody else in the Chennai print market has. |
| **6** | Proves itself. Real work, real clients, real words from them. |

Waves 1–4 are a complete, competitive, premium site. Wave 5 is the one people will remember. **Waves 1–4 first — always.**

# Content Audit - thoorigaiprints.com

Captured 21 August 2026 from a full crawl of `wp-sitemap.xml` (24 pages, 9 blog posts, 7 testimonial records) plus the WordPress REST media library (155 records).

**Platform:** WordPress with The7 theme v14.4.2 and Elementor Pro, served through LiteSpeed cache. Contact Form 7 and Elementor Forms are both installed; only Elementor Forms is used. A Click-to-Chat WhatsApp widget is active site-wide.

## Headline findings

1. **The site is a picture gallery, not a sales site.** Eleven of the seventeen commercial pages carry under 250 characters of body text. Products are shown as an image with a two-word label and nothing else.
2. **Every page is missing a meta description**, and only the homepage has an H1 (it has two).
3. **All 155 images in the media library have empty alt text.**
4. **The blog is entirely Lorem Ipsum** across all nine posts.
5. **All seven testimonials are theme demo content** about an AI course, attributed to invented people with stock portraits. None of them render on the site.
6. **Four orphan pages are publicly indexable**, including a complete AI-course landing page (`/home-ai-2/`) that has nothing to do with printing.
7. **Three different phone numbers** are published across the site.
8. **The eleven child service pages duplicate their parent** page's grid with no unique copy.
9. **No social media presence is linked** from anywhere on the site.
10. The footer's "Privacy Page" link points at `/404`, and the copyright line still reads 2025.
